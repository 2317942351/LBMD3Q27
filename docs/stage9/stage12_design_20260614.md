# Stage12 — W=3 Curved-Wall Static Validation Design

Date: 2026-06-14
Status: authorized (reviewer 2026-06-14). Uses stage9 Briant binary, W=3.
No code change. Static only. theta=90 neutral control required first.

## Goal

Verify that the Stage11 finding (W=3 gives < 2 deg error at theta=30 on a
plane) extends to curved walls: cylinder and sphere.

## Why theta=90 neutral control first

The reviewer's requirement. theta=90 is the trivial case (Briant formula
reduces to ghost = mirror fluid value). If theta=90 is off on a curved wall,
the error is NOT from the wetting formula — it is from post-processing
(contact-angle measurement), geometry (STL/side convention), or the analytic
normal injection. In that case the theta=30 result is uninterpretable and
Stage12 stops.

**theta=90 pass criterion:** measured angle in [88, 92] deg on BOTH cylinder
and sphere. If either fails, Stage12 STOPS and the geometry/post-processing
is debugged before any theta=30 claim.

## Test matrix (4 cases, serial)

All: stage9 Briant binary, W=3, sigma=5e-5, M=0.1, tauUpdate=1, 100k steps,
AnalyticWetting=1.

```
case            geometry         theta   grid              droplet
cyl_theta090    cylinder axis=x  90      96x96x96          R=16 at (48,48,70)
cyl_theta030    cylinder axis=x  30      96x96x96          R=16 at (48,48,70)
sph_theta090    sphere           90      80x80x140         R=16 at (40,40,90)
sph_theta030    sphere           30      80x80x140         R=16 at (40,40,90)
```

Cylinder: axis along x, centered at (cx=48, cy=48) in y-z plane, radius 20.
The cylinder is infinite along x (the STL is a finite tube spanning the
domain; TCLB treats the ends as wall). Droplet sits above the cylinder apex.

Sphere: center (40,40,48), radius 20. Droplet above apex (apex at z=68).
Droplet R=16 at z=90 spans z=74..106, clear of the sphere.

R=16 chosen so R/W = 16/3 ~ 5.3, above the under-resolution threshold (~4W
per Stage11 finding) and large enough for a measurable contact line.

### STL requirements

- Cylinder STL: binary, normals pointing OUTWARD (away from axis), so
  `side="out"` marks the exterior as fluid and the cylinder interior as solid.
- Sphere STL: same convention, outward normals, `side="out"`.
- Both generated by Python (gen_sphere_stl.py for sphere; a new
  gen_cylinder_stl.py for cylinder), with explicit outward-normal enforcement.

## Pass criteria (all 4 required)

```
cyl_theta090: measured angle in [88, 92]   (neutral control)
sph_theta090: measured angle in [88, 92]   (neutral control)
cyl_theta030: measured angle in [28, 32]   (target 30, +/-2)
sph_theta030: measured angle in [28, 32]   (target 30, +/-2)
```

All four must pass. If theta=90 fails on either geometry, STOP (geometry/
post-processing issue). If theta=90 passes but theta=30 fails, the curved-
wall wetting at W=3 has a residual error > 2 deg, and the report says so
honestly (no pass).

## Contact-angle measurement for curved walls

The plane-wall two-row method does not directly apply to curved walls. For
the cylinder and sphere, measure the contact angle from the phi=0.5 contour
in a vertical slice through the axis:

- Find the phi=0.5 contour points near the solid surface.
- Fit the local interface tangent at the contact line.
- The contact angle is the angle between this tangent and the solid surface
  tangent (perpendicular to the analytic normal at the contact point).

This requires a more careful post-processor than the plane two-row method.
The post-processor must be validated on the theta=90 case first (where the
answer is known: 90 deg).

## What Stage12 does NOT do

- No dynamic impact (deferred per reviewer).
- No wall-surface BC redesign (not supported by Stage11 data).
- No R=48 (deferred).
- No theta sweep (30 and 90 only).
- No claim about "all contact angles work" — only theta=30 and 90 on these
  two geometries.

## Risk: W=3 stability on curved walls with STL

Stage11 showed W=3 is stable on a plane. The STL geometry introduces sharper
solid boundaries, which may destabilize the interface at W=3. If any case
NaNs, that is reported as a stability finding (not hidden), and the case is
marked FAILED.
