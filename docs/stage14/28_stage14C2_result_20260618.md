must be understood before any higher M is adopted).

## 3. The t150 drift (the finding)

theta(t) trajectory at M=0.3, t150 (init=150):
```text
150.08 -> 150.29 -> 150.99 -> 151.50 -> 151.99 -> 152.48 -> 152.97
(step 0 -> 2000 -> 4000 -> 6000 -> 8000 -> 10000 -> 12000)
```
- Monotonic increasing, ~+0.5 deg per 2000 steps, **still rising at 12000**.
- Not a fast instability (no NaN, mass drift only +0.13%, RMS modest).
- For comparison, at M=0.1 (doc 25 §2.1) t150 equilibrium was 150.09 deg
  (essentially exact). So raising M from 0.1 to 0.3 introduces a slow obtuse
  over-shoot that did not exist at M=0.1.

This is the classic diffuse-interface trade-off: higher mobility speeds contact-
line kinetics but can let the static angle drift because the interface diffuses
faster than the wetting geometry can pin it. The acute case (t30) is unaffected;
the obtuse case (t150, the most diffuse/spread droplet) is most sensitive.

## 4. Honest correction to the prior recommendation

Doc 27 recommended M=0.3 based ONLY on the decoupled speed (14C-1). That was
incomplete: 14C-1 never checked equilibrium. 14C-2 shows M=0.3 fails the
obtuse equilibrium gate, so:

```text
M=0.3 is NOT acceptable as the working mobility.
```

The decision of working mobility must satisfy BOTH:
```text
(a) decoupled speed gain (14C-1), AND
(b) equilibrium angle preservation across 30/90/150 (14C-2).
```
M=0.3 satisfies (a) but fails (b) at t150.

## 5. Revised plan

### 5a. Run 14C-2 at M=0.2 (and possibly M=0.15)

Re-run equilibrium 30/90/150 at M=0.2. Expectation: t150 drift smaller than at
0.3, hopefully under the 0.5 deg gate. If M=0.2 passes all three, it becomes
the working mobility (it gave +0.59 deg/1k for 120->150 and -0.25 for 60->30
in doc 27 — a real, if smaller, speed gain over M=0.1, without the t150 break).

```text
14C-2b:  equilibrium 30/90/150 @ M=0.2, 12000 steps
gate: |err|<0.5 on ALL THREE (esp. t150)
```

If M=0.2 still fails t150, drop to M=0.15. The working mobility is the highest
M that holds t150 equilibrium.

### 5b. Understand the obtuse drift (separate diagnostic, do not block)

Even at the chosen M, the fact that t150 is the sensitive case is worth a
short diagnostic: is the drift (i) a genuine phase-field diffusion effect
(mobility too high for the spread droplet), (ii) a shape-angle measurement
artefact at obtuse angles (circle-arc fit degenerates as the cap flattens),
or (iii) a compact-ghost angle error that mobility amplifies? This is deferred
but noted.

### 5c. Only after a passing M, run 14C-3

```text
14C-3: decoupled 60->30 / 120->150 @ chosen M, 24000 steps
```
Proceed as in the user's plan, but at the equilibrium-safe M, not M=0.3.
