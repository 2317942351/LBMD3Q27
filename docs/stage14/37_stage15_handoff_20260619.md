## 1. The physics being added (one paragraph)

A residual contact-line force is added to `F_total` to compensate the ~6x
tangential dilution of the compact-ghost wall BC. The force is

```text
F_CL = DynamicCLForceSign · DynamicCLCoeff · (sigma/IntWidth) · R_theta · I_cl · t_CL
       (capped at  DynamicCLForceCap · sigma/IntWidth)
R_theta = cos(theta_eq) - cos(theta_app)        (vanishes at equilibrium by construction)
t_CL    = normalize( n_i - (n_i·n_w) n_w )       (interface normal projected onto wall)
I_cl    = 4 q (1-q)                               (contact-line indicator, 0<=q<=1)
```

It is Mode-gated: Mode=0 off, Mode=1 shadow (diagnostics only), Mode=2 adds
to F_total. The compact ghost stays the sole wall-phase physics channel;
DynamicCL only adds a body force. Design doc: `docs/stage14/31_dynamicCL_residual_force_plan_20260618.md`.

**Do NOT raise ForceCap further** — it would amplify the reverse trend.

### Recommended next steps (in order)

1. **Read the actual force direction** in the 120->150 test VTI. On active CL
   nodes compute the radial projection `(ForceCandidateX·e_rx + ForceCandidateZ·e_rz)`
   (e_r = radial outward in x-z, center 48,48). Desired sign for retract (theta_app
   must increase) is INWARD = negative. If it comes out positive on the obtuse
   case, the force points the wrong way there. Files:
   `/mnt/usb1t/RUNS/runs/stage15C2f_changed_angle_test/decouple_wall_120to150/output/case_VTK_P00_*.vti`
   (use the stage15c1_inspect.py / stage15C2b_locality_probe.py pattern).
2. **Re-derive the C1 sign from EARLY-TIME R_theta** (step 500-1000, large
   residual ~0.1) instead of the step-4000 over-relaxed value (~0.05, narrow
   margin). The C1 sign vote was done on over-relaxed data; it may not hold
   when the force is strong.
3. **Verify the t_CL orientation assumption** on the 150 deg contact line. C1
   only proved "t_CL is horizontal and points radially outward" on the flat-wall
   ring; obtuse contact-line geometry may orient t_CL differently and flip the
   effective force direction.
4. If the direction is confirmed wrong in the obtuse regime, the fix is a
   **sign-convention correction** (DynamicCLForceSign, or a geometry/angle-dependent
   sign term), NOT a coefficient change. Re-do C1 calibration after any such fix.
